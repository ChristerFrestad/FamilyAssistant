The week, on one board

One place for dinner, chores, the kitchen and the shopping list.

Hverdagsplanleggeren is a Norwegian family app that keeps dinner, chores, what is already in the kitchen, and the shopping list together. The list is built from the menu and skips what you already have. Free to start. Your data stays with you.
